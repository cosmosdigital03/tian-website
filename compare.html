What is TIAN?

TIAN is a privacy-first network built around a dual-shield design that separates your connecting identity from your browsing destination—combining seamless privacy routing with intelligent ad and tracker filtering.

How do the two shields work?

Entry handles the device-facing connection and shields your personal IP address. Exit handles connections onward to the internet without seeing your original device IP. This separates the information each shield needs for its role.

Which platforms will TIAN support?

TIAN is coming soon for iPhone, Android, Windows, and Mac. Release announcements and download links will be published right here as each platform launches.

What does “zero browsing logs” mean?

TIAN never records or stores your browsing history, visited websites, traffic content, or individual DNS queries. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

What does TIAN’s filtering block?

Built-in filter lists block connections to known advertising, tracking, and malicious domains across your connection, alongside optional Safari page-level content blocking on iPhone.

Does TIAN replace website account privacy?

TIAN protects your network connection and masks your personal IP address from the websites you visit. When you sign into an account or accept cookies on a website, that site can still recognize your account activity.

How can I reach the TIAN team?

You can reach us anytime at support@tianvpn.com or through our Support page.
