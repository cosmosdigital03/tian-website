Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 · Operator: Ethear Vast LLC (Florida, United States)

No Browsing Logs

TIAN never logs the websites you visit, the content of your traffic, or your DNS queries.

Encrypted & Divided

Your traffic is encrypted on your device and separated across Entry and Exit relay roles.

Zero Data Sales

We never sell user data, build advertising profiles, or embed third-party ad trackers.

1. Who Operates TIAN

TIAN Private Network and tianvpn.com are operated by Ethear Vast LLC, a limited liability company organized in the State of Florida, United States ("TIAN," "we," "us," or "our"). You can reach us for privacy inquiries, data subject requests, or support at support@tianvpn.com.

2. What We Never Log or Collect

When you use the TIAN VPN tunnel and protected DNS resolver, our network services do not record or store:

  • Your browsing history, visited URLs, or destination web pages.
  • The contents or payloads of your encrypted network traffic.
  • Your individual DNS queries or domain lookup histories.
  • Your full payment card number or card security code (all App Store and Google Play billing is handled directly by Apple or Google).

3. Account, Subscription & Device Information

To authenticate your access, manage your subscription entitlement, and secure your connection, our account system processes:

  • Account & Recovery Email: Your email address is stored for sign-in, verification, account recovery, and customer support. While the app displays an Account ID on your main screen for visual privacy, your email remains linked to your account in our authentication system.
  • Device & Tunnel Authorization: Device registration identifiers, public WireGuard peer keys, app version, filter preferences, domain exceptions, and account status timestamps.
  • Subscription Entitlements: Verified transaction references, plan type, purchase environment, and expiration dates provided by Apple App Store or Google Play to confirm active paid access.

4. Network Routing & Short-Term Operational Telemetry

  • Separated Relay Roles: Your device encrypts traffic before it leaves your phone. Entry relays process transient source packet routing state in memory to forward encrypted traffic without inspecting inner destination payloads. Exit gateways decrypt the inner tunnel, resolve permitted DNS requests, apply enabled domain-blocking filters, and connect to destination websites without seeing your personal device IP.
  • Short-Term Operational & Capacity Metrics: To maintain server stability, prevent credential abuse, and monitor network health, our operational tooling records short-lived rolling metadata consisting of active session intervals, selected gateway regions, and aggregate upload/download byte volumes. This metadata never contains browsing destinations, DNS queries, packet contents, or raw private keys. Rolling device bandwidth buckets are automatically pruned within 24 hours, and activity interval observations are capped at a maximum of 7 days.
  • Disconnected IP Check: When you view your connection state in the app while disconnected from the VPN, the app requests your current public IP address from an external IP-lookup endpoint so you can verify your network state.

5. Infrastructure & Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on vetted infrastructure providers strictly to deliver and secure the Service:

  • Cloud Relay Infrastructure: Vetted cloud hosting providers operate our encrypted Entry and Exit server nodes across regional data centers.
  • Authentication & Database Services: Managed backend authentication infrastructure processes sign-in sessions, access tokens, and gateway security events.
  • Upstream Encrypted DNS (DoH): Permitted DNS queries that pass through our Exit gateway filters are forwarded over encrypted HTTPS (DNS-over-HTTPS) to an upstream resolver (such as Cloudflare) using the Exit gateway's IP address rather than your personal IP address.
  • App Distribution & Billing: Apple and Google process app installations, device integrity checks, and subscription billing under their respective platform policies.

We do not sell user data, and we prohibit our infrastructure processors from using TIAN user data for their own advertising or marketing.

6. Website Privacy (tianvpn.com)

tianvpn.com is a static informational website. Our pages contain zero advertising pixels, third-party analytics scripts, cookies, or browser local-storage trackers. Standard web server delivery logs (such as requesting IP address, browser user-agent, and timestamp) are processed transiently to deliver pages and defend against automated attacks.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Account and device registration records are kept only while your account remains active. You can request permanent account deletion directly inside the TIAN app or by emailing support@tianvpn.com.

When you delete your account, your profile, email record, and relay authorization keys are immediately revoked from our active systems, and residual encrypted database backups or security audit logs cycle out within 30 days (except where limited transaction records must be retained to comply with tax, accounting, or legal obligations).

Note on App Store Subscriptions: Deleting your TIAN account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Please cancel your subscription in your device's account settings before deleting your TIAN account.

8. Your Privacy Rights & Lawful Requests

Depending on your location (including the EEA, UK, California, and other U.S. states), you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete the personal account data we hold about you. To exercise your rights, contact support@tianvpn.com.

We review legal requests carefully and disclose information only when compelled by a valid, binding court order or legal process under applicable law. Because TIAN never logs browsing histories, traffic content, or DNS queries, we cannot produce browsing records under any circumstances.

9. Protection Context & Policy Updates

While TIAN separates network routing roles and filters known tracking domains, signed-in accounts, browser cookies, and device-level telemetry can still identify you to websites and apps. We will post any material updates to this Privacy Policy on this page with an updated Effective Date.

Website privacy

The following information applies to this coming-soon website.

Visiting these pages

This is a static informational website. Its pages do not include advertising pixels, analytics scripts, account sign-in, payment forms, or waitlist forms. The site code does not set cookies or use browser storage. This site uses system fonts and locally included illustrations.

Basic technical request information, including your IP address, browser information, and the time of access, is processed to deliver these pages and help keep them secure. Those website-request records are separate from the VPN’s browsing-history statement.

Your automatic connection check

The homepage automatically shows the public IP, network provider, network owner and ID, and approximate area associated with the connection used for the lookup. It also shows locally available device details and an explanation of any recognized VPN, relay, or server network. JavaScript must be enabled for the check to run.

When the homepage loads, your browser connects directly to IPWHOIS.io, which receives your public IP and the standard request information your browser sends, including browser information and the website origin. The lookup uses HTTPS without cookies, credentials, or a referring-page URL. IPWHOIS handles requests under its own privacy policy. Its processing and retention are separate from TIAN.

Device family, a model when available, browser, operating system, screen size, language, and time zone are calculated locally when the check starts. Where supported, the browser may supply an additional device-model hint; it can also withhold or generalize these details. The website does not upload this on-screen collection of device results, create a fingerprint or tracking ID, or save the check results to cookies, browser storage, or a TIAN database.

The snapshot loads automatically, without a refresh or clear button. Leaving the page clears the displayed results and cancels any pending lookup. Returning through browser history, returning to the page after switching away, or a browser-reported network change refreshes the snapshot, subject to a short cooldown and provider availability. An offline connection clears old network details and the check runs again when the browser reconnects. Not every VPN switch is detectable by a website. There is no periodic polling. Leaving the page does not remove request information already processed by a service provider.

Network recognition happens in the page using the lookup’s network name and ID, a configured list of TIAN exit addresses, and a local copy of Apple’s published Private Relay ranges. Visitors do not contact Apple for this match. Address lists have an expiry; an expired or unmatched list does not establish that a privacy service is absent. Recognized third-party VPNs and relays receive the same positive treatment as recognized TIAN exits.

A server address, including one registered to Amazon or DigitalOcean, may belong to a VPN, proxy, or another server-based connection. An ordinary internet-provider address may also be used by a VPN. These results identify the IP route and database information available to this lookup; they do not verify VPN encryption, DNS handling, leak prevention, or protection for other apps. HTTPS is shown separately for the connection to this website. The estimated area comes from the public IP, not GPS, and device identification may be approximate. The check does not collect VPN browsing history.
