Website privacy.

Updated October 7, 2026. This page describes the TIAN coming-soon website. It is not the privacy policy for the TIAN app or VPN service.

Visiting the website

This is a static informational website. Its pages do not include advertising pixels, analytics scripts, account sign-in, payment forms, or waitlist forms. The site code does not set cookies or use browser storage.

The hosting provider processes network requests to deliver and protect the website. These requests may include your IP address, browser information, and the date and time of access. The absence of analytics on these pages does not mean the hosting provider keeps no operational records.

Contacting support

Email links open your email app. If you choose to send a message to support@tianvpn.com, TIAN and its email service provider receive the address, message, and attachments you send so your request can be handled.

Only include information needed for your question. Do not send passwords, private keys, payment details, or browsing history.

Questions

For questions about information you have sent to TIAN, including requests concerning that information, email support@tianvpn.com.

Before using the app

Review the privacy information supplied with the applicable TIAN app release before creating an account or using the VPN service. Website information alone does not describe app account data, VPN operations, or service-provider retention.
