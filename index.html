What is TIAN?

TIAN is a private network in development for iPhone. Its two-shield design separates the entry and exit roles of your internet connection.

Why two shields?

Identity Shield handles the incoming connection. Destination Shield handles the outbound connection. Separating these roles is designed to limit the information available at either relay.

When can I use it?

TIAN is coming soon. There is no public launch date yet. We’ll share availability details here when they’re ready.

Will TIAN make me anonymous?

No VPN makes you anonymous everywhere. Accounts you sign into, cookies, and browser characteristics can still identify you to websites. Read our privacy information for more context.
