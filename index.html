Ask us something
What is TIAN?

TIAN is a private network in development for iPhone. Its two-shield design separates the entry and exit roles of your internet connection.

Why two shields?

Identity Shield / Entry handles the device-facing connection. Destination Shield / Exit handles connections onward to the internet. The device-to-Exit inner connection remains encrypted as Entry forwards it. This separates the information each relay needs for its role.

Can I download TIAN now?

TIAN is in development for iPhone. We’ll share the public launch date and download details here when they’re ready.

Does a private route make me anonymous?

A private network route separates network information. Signed-in accounts, cookies, and browser characteristics are separate ways websites recognize you. Both TIAN relays are part of one service, and traffic analysis can still correlate activity. Our privacy overview explains this context.

What does “no browsing-history logs” mean?

It refers to browsing-history, content, and DNS-query logs retained by TIAN’s own VPN services. Account, device, support, and essential operational records are separate. External service providers handle the information needed to deliver their services under their own policies. See the full scope.

What can TIAN’s filters block?

Enabled domain lists help block connections to known advertising, tracking, and malicious domains. Coverage varies by website and app. Optional Safari content blocking adds page-request filtering and supported element hiding in Safari on iPhone. It does not add Safari’s page-level filtering to Chrome or other apps.

How can I contact support?

Email support@tianvpn.com. Existing testers can include their app build, iOS version, and the error shown. Please leave out passwords, recovery codes, payment details, and browsing history.
