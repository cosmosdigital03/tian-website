Ask us something
What is TIAN?

TIAN is a private network in development for iPhone. Its two-shield design separates the entry and exit roles of your internet connection.

Why two shields?

Identity Shield handles the incoming connection. Destination Shield handles the outbound connection. Separating the roles is designed to limit what is available at either relay. Both shields are part of TIAN’s infrastructure.

Can I download TIAN now?

There is no public launch date or download yet. We’ll share availability details here when they’re ready. This website doesn’t take payments or collect waitlist sign-ups.

Does a private route make me anonymous?

No VPN makes you anonymous everywhere. Accounts you sign into, cookies, and browser characteristics can still identify you to websites. Read our privacy overview for more context.

What does “no browsing-history logs” mean?

It refers to browsing-history, content, and DNS-query logs retained by TIAN’s own VPN services. Account, device, support, and essential operational records are separate. Upstream providers, including the default Cloudflare DNS resolver, follow their own policies. See the full scope.

How can I contact support?

Email support@tianvpn.com. Existing testers can include their app build, iOS version, and the error shown. Please leave out passwords, recovery codes, payment details, and browsing history.
