01 / YOUR DEVICE

It starts
with you.

Every connection begins on your device. TIAN sends it through two separate shields, so neither side holds the full picture of who you are and where you are going.

02 / ENTRY SHIELD · WHO YOU ARE

Where you connect.
Protected from the start.

The Identity Shield receives your connection and masks your personal IP address at the point of entry—without visibility into the websites or services you visit.

Your personal IP address stops here and never reaches your destination.

03 / TWO POINTS

Two separate
views.

One seamless connection, divided into two separate points of view. For your protection, the Entry shield knows who you are. The Exit shield knows where you are going. Neither holds both in one single place.

04 / EXIT SHIELD · WHERE YOU GO

Where you’re going.
Completely separated.

The Destination Shield connects you onward to the web and applies intelligent domain filtering—without knowing your original device IP address.

Websites see the Destination Shield address, never your personal connection.

05 / THE OPEN INTERNET

Then out to
the open internet.

Built-in domain filtering blocks known advertising, tracking, and malicious hosts before they load—giving you a cleaner, quieter connection across your apps.
