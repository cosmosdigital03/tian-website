Have a question?

Contact support@tianvpn.com with general questions or, if you’re an existing tester, a description of the issue you’re seeing.

For troubleshooting, include your app build number, iOS version, and the message shown in the app. Remove personal information from any screenshots.

Please don’t send passwords, verification links, recovery codes, private keys, payment information, or browsing history.

About the two-shield design

Identity Shield is the entry relay. Destination Shield is the exit relay. Both are part of TIAN’s infrastructure; separating their roles is designed to reduce the information available at either relay.

A VPN does not make you anonymous everywhere. Websites may still recognize accounts you sign into, cookies, or browser characteristics.
