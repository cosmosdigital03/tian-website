6. Service Availability & Technical Limits

Connection speeds, latency, server locations, and domain-filtering coverage naturally vary across networks and regions. While TIAN separates device-facing and destination-facing relay roles and blocks known advertising and tracking domains via enabled filter lists, no VPN or filter can guarantee 100% anonymity, unblockable access to every website or regional streaming catalog, or removal of every advertisement or threat. Signed-in third-party accounts, browser cookies, and device-level telemetry operate independently of network routing.
