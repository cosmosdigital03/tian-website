PRIVACY, IN DETAIL

Clear by design.

A private connection starts with a clear understanding of what happens to your information.

Updated October 19, 2026

Browsing privacy

TIAN’s own VPN services do not retain browsing-history, content, or DNS-query logs. This is the scope of “No browsing-history logs” on this website.

Account, device, support, and essential operational records are handled separately. These can include authentication and security information, device records, support messages, and aggregate diagnostics.

Service providers process information needed to deliver their services under their own policies. TIAN’s default upstream DNS resolver is Cloudflare. Its public DNS resolver policy describes the information it processes and retains, including limited resolver logs and sampled or aggregate data. This processing is separate from the browsing-history records covered by TIAN’s statement.

TIAN uses nested WireGuard tunnels with device-facing Entry and destination-facing Exit roles. Both relays are part of TIAN. Signed-in accounts, cookies, and browser characteristics remain ways websites recognize you, and traffic analysis can still correlate activity.

TIAN is in development. This overview is not the complete privacy policy for an app release or the VPN service. Before creating an account or using TIAN, review the privacy information supplied with that release.

Website privacy

The following information applies to this coming-soon website.

Visiting these pages

This is a static informational website. Its pages do not include advertising pixels, analytics scripts, account sign-in, payment forms, or waitlist forms. The site code does not set cookies or use browser storage. This site uses system fonts and locally included illustrations.

Basic technical request information, including your IP address, browser information, and the time of access, is processed to deliver these pages and help keep them secure. Those website-request records are separate from the VPN’s browsing-history statement.

Your local browser preview

The homepage’s “What your connection reveals” preview reads basic browser information only when you choose “Show browser details.” It displays the browser, estimated device type and operating system, screen dimensions, browser language, and time zone on your screen. These values can be limited, changed, or misreported by browser settings; time zone does not establish your location.

The preview code does not transmit these displayed details, store them in cookies or browser storage, or use them to create a tracking identifier. Choosing “Hide browser details” clears the displayed values. IP address, approximate location, and internet provider are unavailable because no network lookup is configured. The preview makes no external lookup or GPS request and does not determine whether a VPN is active or your connection is secure.

This local preview is separate from the technical website-request information described above. No website traffic analytics collection or 30-day analytics retention system is currently enabled.

Contacting support

Email links open your email app. If you send a message to support@tianvpn.com, TIAN and its email service provider receive the address, message, and attachments you send so your request can be handled.

Only include information needed for your question. Do not send passwords, private keys, payment details, or browsing history.

Privacy questions

For questions or requests about information you have sent to TIAN, email support@tianvpn.com.
